Selected Projects



📁 Ezra – A Task Management App

Productivity Task Management MVP

Role: Product Manager

Overview: Ezra is a clean, intuitive task management tool for users who crave simplicity without losing power. I led user research, MVP scoping, and feature prioritization to strike this balance.

  1. Conducted 10 interviews & 50 surveys to identify pain points.
  2. Defined key personas and used MoSCoW to prioritize features.
  3. Scoped MVP with task management, notes, progress tracking, dark mode.
  4. Set KPIs focused on adoption, retention, and task completion.

Impact: Ezra stands out by combining clarity with essential productivity features—no bloat.
